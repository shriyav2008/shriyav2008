01 Shipped

A music player

A player with play and pause, a progress bar and a skip, holding two songs: “Sunflower” by Post Malone and “She’s All I Wanna Be” by Tate McRae.

What it taught me

Keeping the interface in step with the track was the lesson. The button, the bar and the title all have to agree about what is playing, and most of my bugs were the three disagreeing.

03 Newest

A photobooth

Started as a hand-tracked game and became a photobooth. The webcam feeds a canvas, you pick a filter, and it counts down and takes a strip of three photos.

What it taught me

Working with a camera means handling permission, a live preview and a canvas that draws on top of the frame, and being kind to people who say no.
